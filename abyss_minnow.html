HOW TO PLAY

Master the ecosystem of Abyss Minnow

🧭

Fluid 360° Swimming & Dash

Steer freely with WASD, Arrows, or your Mouse Pointer (touch & drag on mobile). Press SPACE, SHIFT, or Right-Click to burst forward using Dash Energy.

✨

Eat, Combo & Evolve

Devour glowing plankton, krill, and fish smaller than you to grow across 5 Evolution Stages. Eat rapidly within 3.5 seconds to chain a Feeding Streak up to x8 score!

🦈

Predators & Near-Misses

Larger creatures glow with a crimson/amber danger rim and flash their eyes before lunging. Narrowly dodging a predator awards a Near-Miss Bonus (+150) and restores Dash Energy.

🌊

Ocean Currents, Pearls & Depth Zones

Ride glowing Current Ribbons for speed boosts, hunt rare Golden Shimmerfish & Bioluminescent Pearls, and dive deeper to discover 4 unique ocean zones for exploration bonuses.
